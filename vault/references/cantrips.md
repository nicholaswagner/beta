## Quick Build Recommendations

For a **new Wizard**, prioritize these in order:

1. **Shield** — free reaction defense, no save check needed
2. **Detect Magic** — essential adventuring utility
3. **Message** — party communication at range
4. **Prestidigitation** — infinite utility tricks
5. **Electric Arc** or **Frostbite** — reliable attack cantrip (pick element you like)
6. **Light** — illumination when needed
7. **Read Aura** or **Approximate** — focused detection

Then pick remaining slots based on your build theme (elemental focus, illusion, necromancy, etc.) or situations you expect to face.
